# 2026-10-18 -- Step caps

Status: APPROVED (owner, 2026-10-18). Registry: `stages.yaml` `caps:` block (builder A).
Audit: `docs/audit/2026-10-18_step_caps/STEP_CAPS.md` (29 episode folders, records read by name).

## Why

No step had a wall on its first pass; the 5 h ceiling bounded only ladder rungs. The plan
looped at the episode level: a battery refusal deleted `plan.json` and the next run
re-authored, with drive x6, relaunch x2 and brain x3 each restarting the count. Jekyll
Kawajiri ep01 lost about 45 min (69-79 refusals on voice-measured rows); WotW ep15 ran 24
plan climbs and 301 min. A killed step left no timing row.

## Decision

- Every step has `rounds`, `per` (pass, climb, round, draw, grid, take, episode) and
  `wall_min`; the full table is in `.claude/skills/episode/GATES.md` "Step caps".
- At its cap a step keeps its best attempt, logs
  `CAP <phase>: r/N rounds, mm:ss, kept best (k advisories)` and goes on. Never defer, never
  re-author past the table, never park on style.
- Plan: 3 writer rounds per climb inside 30 min, at most 2 climbs per episode; a fault set
  that is tier 1 only parks once (one tier-1 re-author per episode).
- Caps marked `episode` (voice, regrid, retakes, qc, master_eye) count across resumes and
  relaunches.
- A step logs `STEP <id> start` (Seattle time) on entry.
- The 6 h episode clock stays the outer wall. The walls sum to about 8.5 h; they stop a
  runaway step, not a normal one. The share column sums to 359 min.
