## What changed in the org (architecture)

- **Take ladder rule (episode/09):** a rung returns `RungResult(changed, reason, seconds)` (K11);
  no change is `NO ROUND`; the router reads dated cure rates; every kept fault is stamped
  `settled` (K5); the batch is trimmed to what the budget affords.
- **Ship policy (episode/10):** a settled keep_best take ships flagged and is listed in
  `takes/work/flagged_takes.json` (K7); G-DEAD refuses only frozen rows and unsettled fails.
- **Shared services:** the family-boundary free (`comfy.free_on_family`, K10, called by
  `step_runner` from the stages.yaml `family:` key, K2); the step-wall heartbeat
  (`step_wall.Heartbeat`, `step_attempts.elapsed`, K3); `run_clock.dead_gaps` (K4); the owner-order
  allowance (`work_orders.recorded`, K12); the GPU ledger (`scripts/episode/gpu_ledger.py`,
  `library/.device/gpu_util.jsonl`); the VRAM-first router with claim liveness.
- **Studio office:** the autopilot hands over to one detached successor per HEAD, never asks the
  brain on a dirty tree or a moved HEAD, un-parks a false brain park when runtime code lands,
  watches ComfyUI's health and a stuck step by CPU and output growth.
- **Plan (episode/02):** real rows, a climb spent at the first writer call, the re-sign over
  carried rows, `desk.stuck`; the advisory downstream replay at plan sign
  (`scripts/episode/downstream_replay.py`).
- **Board (episode/07-08):** solid figures under haze; a repaired anchor keeps its siblings when the
  place did not move; the panel eye's proven cache, deadline and `eye_unread.json`.
- **Master (episode/10-11):** the frame invariant (`ShortPass`), the av3 pace guard, the master-eye
  read cache, the edit gate on the dead cut.
- **Command Center:** the live card folds a step's history across rewinds and prints the debt.
- **Docs:** the cap, algorithm and clock tables are generated from the registry
  (`scripts/episode/commands.py`); LESSONS split into an index and an archive;
  `scripts/audit/doc_sync.py`.
- **Not built, Future tab:** the facenet / EasyOCR torch worker; the one event stream (C1, C2, C5).
