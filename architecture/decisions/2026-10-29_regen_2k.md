## What it is

Open-H3-Regenerate-2K (NVIDIA Sana, 2026-10-27): Kijai's extracted rank-384 refiner LoRA on Comfy-Org's
pruned ref2va int8 (the only base with PDD head banks, which the single-head LoRA needs on 0.43, PR 17239),
LMS 0.8, CK int8 attention, 4 sa_solver steps at denoise 0.35, shift 12/3. Each take is enlarged 2x on the
CPU and injected (H3InjectVideoLatent); the conditioning is the take's OWN references (cast sheets + panel).
Graph: comfy_studio `video_minimax_h3_regen2k_lms_kijai_visurena` (new file).

## What changed

1. **Step 11, before the one upscale** (`scripts/episode/regen_2k.py`, `studio/regen_2k.py`): every non-still
   take of the judged cut, people first, is re-drawn unless its regen is current (takes/r2v_2k/regen.json
   records the take sha8 it answers). Spends min(max(6 h - spent - 20 min reserve, a 60 min floor), the
   `regen_2k` cap row 200 min) -- the 6 h is a target, not a stop (owner 2026-10-26);
   a take it cannot afford keeps av3.
2. **The reference gate**: a take whose faces (MTCNN, five frames, >= 16 px at 768) outnumber its sheets is
   not drawn; av3 keeps it. Crowds: a masked pass (regen everything, paste unreferenced faces back from the
   take) is the next build.
3. **The splice** (`regen_2k.splice`, from `assemble.upscale_cut`): the av3 picture with each drawn shot's
   frames in its place, frame for frame (the segment rebuilt the way the cut built it: head, G-DEAD slow,
   gutter crop at 2x, fill to 3072), encoded once; the cut's sound copied; `cut/upscaled.json` traces it.
4. **The face refine is switched off** while the pass is on (`studio/face_refine.eligible`), kept, not
   deleted: `VISURENA_REGEN_2K=0` brings the refine and the av3-only master back.
