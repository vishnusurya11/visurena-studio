## What it is

A take gate row, `warp` (`studio/warp_gate.py`), measured by `take_dq` on every take that stages a face -- so it rides
the CPU lane beside the renders (`take_dq --cpu-only`, `studio/take_overlap.py`) and the GPU never waits on it.

- Faces: MTCNN every 2nd frame (min 24 px, prob >= 0.9), linked by IoU > 0.3; a track swap is a new track, never a fault.
- Flow: Farneback forward and backward at 384 px; the warp residual counts only pixels where the two agree (< 1.5 px).
- E(t) = face residual - (2.51 x background residual + 1.69 x face flow + 1.00); the background excludes the body.
- S = p90 of E; Se = p90 over the eye mask; share = searched frames with a tracked face.

| state | when | cure |
|---|---|---|
| FAIL (hard row) | S > 2.5 or Se > 3.0 | the ladder's `seed` (take_ladder.cure_of), at most 2 retakes (ROUNDS_CAP), then F2 |
| WARN | 1.5 < S <= 2.5 | F2 face refine, whatever the shot size |
| UNMEASURABLE | face tracked on < 45 % of searched frames | F2 face refine |

F2 (`studio/face_refine.eligible`) now also takes a take whose only hard failure is the `warp` row, once its retakes
are spent (`take_ladder.attempts_of`).
