# Can the ref2v take render go faster, and better? (2026-09-16)

Question from the owner: can FastVideo's FastH3-8-Step-V2 or the TaoMate H3
runtime speed up the episode ref2v workflow, and is there anything else that
raises speed or quality.

## What the workflow is today

`scripts/episode/takes_r2v.py` builds `video_minimax_h3_r2v_turbo_ref8` from
`comfy_studio/workflows/video`:

| Node | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| UNETLoader | `minimax_h3_ref2va_pruned_fp8_scaled` (20 GB) |
| LoraLoaderModelOnly | lightx2v `ref2v_turbo_8step_v1.0_768p` at 1.0 |
| PathchSageAttentionKJ | `disabled`, `allow_compile` false |
| CLIPLoader | `qwen3vl_32b_minimax_h3_nvfp4_awq` (15 GB) |
| Sampler | euler, beta, 8 steps, shift 12 / 3, cfg via BasicGuider |
| Canvas | 768 x 1344 (9:16), 24 fps, 124-209 frames per take |
| Guides | `MiniMaxH3AddGuide` per pinned cell + the take's wav |

Machine: RTX 4090 24 GB, 64 GB RAM, torch 2.11.0+cu130, ComfyUI 2026-09-11,
launched with `--reserve-vram 2`. Attention: **pytorch SDPA**. The installed
`sageattention` wheel is `cu128torch2.10.0andhigher.post5` on a cu130 torch, so
it fails to import (`_fused DLL load failed`) and the KJ node is left disabled.

## Measured: where a take's time goes (episode 10, today)

From ComfyUI's own log timestamps, 13 takes of 124-209 frames:

| Phase | Seconds | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| text encode + reference encode | 4-6 | 2 % |
| DiT sampling, 8 steps | 139-215 | 70 % |
| video + audio VAE decode | 25-52 | 15-25 % |
| whole prompt | 160-277 (median ~215) | |

First take after a model swap: 525 s (disk load of TE and DiT). Sampling is
17-27 s per step. Sampling is the lever; decode is second; encode is noise.

## FastH3-8-Step-V2: not usable for this workflow

- Model card and the ComfyUI tutorial both say: **text-to-video and
  first/last-frame only. Ref2VA was not distilled.** Our takes are ref2va with
  up to nine reference pictures plus pixel and audio guides.
- It is a full 35B checkpoint (int8 convrot in ComfyUI form), fixed 8 steps,
  `res_multistep` + `simple`, shift 10 / 3, VSA sparse attention at 80 %
  sparsity. FastVideo's own note: "difficult motion, fine detail, and some
  audio may remain below the base MiniMax H3 model" and it is positioned for
  drafts.
- Verdict: no speed for us, and a quality step down even where it applies.
  Revisit only if FastVideo ships a Ref2VA distillation.

## TaoMate-H3 (Alibaba TaoLive): not usable on this machine

- A streaming runtime, not a ComfyUI workflow. Hopper/SM90 only, validated on
  8 x H20 96 GB, needs 4 or 8 GPUs with tensor + sequence parallelism.
- Its 3-step LoRA is for text/image conditioning. One independent tester
  (matsuo-koya, RTX 5090) tried it for reference + audio lip sync and measured
  identity 0.53 -> 0.42 and lip correlation flipping negative at 832x480; the
  "gains" at 1280x704 came from the model drawing subjects larger and facing
  camera, ignoring the scene. That is the opposite of what a storyboard-driven
  take needs.
- Verdict: no.

## What CAN speed up the current workflow

Ranked by expected gain per unit of risk. None of these was run today: the
4090 was at 100 % rendering episode 10 the whole time.

1. **Turn attention on. Two options, both already on disk or one wheel away.**
   - SageAttention 2.2: replace the cu128 wheel with
     `sageattention-2.2.0+cu130torch2.10.0andhigher.post6-cp310-abi3-win_amd64.whl`
     from woct0rdho's Windows releases (stable ABI; the torch 2.11 + CUDA 13
     import bug, issue 89, was fixed 2026-03-25, before post6 shipped
     2026-07-17). Then flip node 3 to `sageattn_qk_int8_pv_fp16_cuda` per
     workflow, no launch flag. Measured elsewhere: 1.37x on the sampler
     (RTX 3090, 9.00 -> 6.57 s/it) at 30 dB PSNR against SDPA.
   - Comfy Kitchen int8 attention: `comfy_kitchen.int8_attention_is_available()`
     returns True in this python. Enabled by the `--use-ck-attention` launch
     flag (global, needs a restart). Measured on a 4090: 1.34x whole prompt at
     124 frames, 1.79x on the sampler at 328 frames. No gain reported on
     Blackwell, which is why the Comfy-Org thread is lukewarm; on Ada it is
     the bigger of the two reports.
   - Expected on our 215 s take: sampling 139-215 s -> roughly 90-150 s,
     whole take about 25-35 % shorter. Quality must be checked by the DQ gate
     on one take before it goes into the run: int8 QK is a real, small
     numerical change.

2. **A/B the pruned int8 convrot ref2va unet against fp8_scaled** (both on
   disk, 21 GB each). On CUDA 13, pruned int8 convrot beat pruned fp8 by 8-30 %
   on a 5090 (192 frames, 25 steps, gap shrinking with resolution); a 4090
   report of fp8 being 1.38x faster was on CUDA 12.8, which the same author
   says is the degraded path for int8. We are on cu130. Convrot int8 is also
   generally the closer-to-bf16 quantization, so this may be a quality win as
   well. One take, same seed, both unets, compare seconds and the DQ report.

3. **Do not add step caches.** Spectrum and EasyCache skip transformer calls
   by predicting the next step from the last; at 8 distilled steps adjacent
   steps are not similar enough. pepikir measured EasyCache's default as a
   crosshatch weave and saturated chroma, and the safe setting keeps only
   1.14x. Not worth a gate failure.

4. **Decode is 15-25 % and fixed.** The video VAE is a 256-px tiled ViT;
   larger tiles break output (PSNR 40 -> 21 dB, matsuo-koya). Canvas is pinned
   by H3's own `adapt_canvas`. Nothing to take here without a new VAE.

5. **Already on:** the Comfy model compiler (log shows "graph breaks: 3-4,
   rogues: 0"), native fp8 scaled_mm on sm89 through the quantization
   metadata path, text encoder and DiT swapped through dynamic VRAM loading in
   4 s. `--fast fp16_accumulation` is irrelevant to an fp8 unet.

## What could raise quality

- **Alibaba PAI PDD Acc Ref2VA 8-step** (`MiniMax-H3-Ref2VA-Acc-8Step`, on
  disk since 2026-08-27). Official 8-step distillation with a parallel-decoding
  head bank. Needs the `ComfyUI-MiniMax-H3-PDD-Acc` node pack: a plain
  LoraLoader silently drops the head bank. Wiring: euler on the node's own
  sigmas, cfg 1.0, shift exactly 12 / 3, works on pruned fp8 unets. No
  published quality number against the lightx2v LoRA either way, and no word
  on `MiniMaxH3AddGuide` compatibility, so it is a one-take A/B, not a switch.
- **Shift.** The lightx2v release post says 8 steps, shift 12 / 3, euler. The
  ComfyUI-wiki writeup says it was TRAINED at 6 / 3. The workflow runs 12 / 3
  and the code comments record that iteration 3 ran 6 / 3. No new evidence
  today; leave it unless a DQ finding points at motion.
- **No newer ref2v LoRA exists.** lightx2v's repo holds ref2v v0.1 (4-step)
  and v1.0 (8-step) only; fl2v went to v1.2 on 2026-09-03. Community DARETIES
  merges by silveroxides exist but are experiments with mixed reports.

## Proposed experiment, when the GPU is free

One take (T02, 158 frames), same seed, five renders, each through the DQ gate:

| Run | Change |
| --- | --- |
| A | as today (baseline seconds + DQ) |
| B | Sage `qk_int8_pv_fp16_cuda` via node 3 |
| C | `--use-ck-attention` launch flag |
| D | int8 convrot pruned unet, SDPA |
| E | winner of B/C + winner of A/D |

Cost: about 20 minutes of GPU, no credits. Report seconds per phase and the
DQ verdicts side by side.
