# Skill: profile writer — character & location profiles (analysis 05)

You write a production profile from an evidence dossier that a pipeline assembled.
You have NOT read the book and must not pretend to: everything you write comes from
the dossier — its journey (scene-by-scene), state changes, quotes, and companions.
Where the dossier is silent, say so; never invent.

These profiles feed real production work: audiobook narration, image generation for
video, song writing. Concrete detail beats elegant prose.

## Character profile

- `physical`: appearance as the evidence supports — build, age impression, dress,
  distinguishing features. **Write only what the evidence states or clearly implies;
  put anything you are inferring in `inferred_notes`.**
- `physical_evolution`: how their body/appearance/condition changes across the story,
  as a list of `{chapter, change}` drawn from state changes and journey events
  (injuries, exhaustion, illness, ageing, wardrobe shifts).
- `mental`: temperament, intelligence, emotional register, moral outlook.
- `mental_evolution`: how their inner state changes across the story
  `{chapter, change}` — grief, obsession hardening, hope collapsing, resolve forming.
- `voice`: how they speak — diction, rhythm, formality, accent hints, catchphrases.
  Base it on the quotes in the dossier.
- `motivation`: what they want, and what they will do to get it.
- `arc`: one paragraph — where they begin, what changes them, where they end.
- `role_in_story`: their narrative function in one line.
- `inferred_notes`: anything you concluded rather than read. Keep this honest.

## Location profile

- `visual`: what the place looks like — architecture, materials, scale, condition,
  light. This will be used to generate images, so be concrete and specific.
- `atmosphere`: the mood it carries in the story.
- `significance`: why this place matters to the plot.
- `evolution`: how the place or its meaning changes across the story
  `{chapter, change}` (a home becomes a crime scene; a refuge becomes a trap).
- `sensory`: sounds, smells, textures the evidence supports.
- `inferred_notes`: what you inferred rather than read.

## Discipline

- Every claim should be traceable to the dossier. If the dossier gives you three
  scenes, do not write a biography.
- Empty is a valid answer: an evolution list can be `[]` if nothing changed.
- Never contradict the dossier's chapter ordering.
