# Lessons -- the index

The rules under the episode line, brick first (D17, 2026-10-19). Each line is the rule, then the
measurement that made it, then where the full story sits in `LESSONS_ARCHIVE.md`. Open the archive
at the anchor only. A new lesson goes into the archive with its evidence and, when it is a new
brick or a new instance of one, a line here.

## The bricks

**The face debate** (2026-10-21): the 32 px H3 token is the eye's floor; measure, not price -> The face debate.

1. **A gate that measured nothing has passed nothing.** Empty sets, missing inputs and unread
   pictures refuse (2026-09-22 audit). -> Process; DQ.
2. **A round, climb or cap is spent only when it changed bytes** (B1): never for a call, a
   re-sign, a re-judge or a relaunch. ep01: 2 retake rounds booked at 0.0 s, 3 climbs for 2
   writer entries, 28 panels re-read for 2 changed. -> Jekyll Kawajiri ep01 retro, failures 1, 6, 7.
3. **A gate is only as true as the population it was fitted on** (B2). A wall with no owner
   labels is advisory, a HARD row states its outcome at the cap, and a ruler written for one plan
   form never judges another: replay every downstream gate on a new form, no write, before the
   launch. ep01: an Se wall fitted on 27 unlabelled clips failed 8 takes; pair rulers refused a
   grammar plan; a photo sharpness measure scored every cel refine down. -> ep01 retro, failures
   2, 3, 7, 8; Jekyll Kawajiri ep01 under the step caps; The face refine, round 2.
4. **One rule per fact, read by every channel; a number lives in one home** (B3). ep01: a setup
   crowd leaked through 5 channels into 14 takes; the 8.0 s take budget had 4 copies. A freeze
   must hold at EVERY place that renders, and `tests/test_every_take_writer_holds_the_freeze.py`
   lists them: a new writer of `T<nn>.mp4` fails it until it names its freeze site; a launch runs
   the dry check, which calls the deciders, never a counter; a round counts on bytes its own run
   landed. -> ep01 retro, failures 3, 4; A freeze holds; The ep01 deadline retro.
5. **A gate that counts cannot see shape; a repair re-stages only what it changed** (B4). ep01:
   a fading body and a floating hand passed every gate; grids 13-17 were redrawn for one figure.
   -> ep01 retro, failure 5.
6. **A clock not charged when its process dies is not a clock; liveness is CPU plus output
   growth, never a quiet log** (B5). ep01: the av3 hang went uncharged (wall.10 = 11 s for ~41
   min), ComfyUI died unseen. -> ep01 retro, failures 9-13.
7. **One ruler.** A judge and the cure for its fault measure the same number the same way; a
   fault no cure can move is a ruler mismatch until both numbers are printed. ep21: a cure at the
   rate against a judge by the voice looped. -> The autopilot under the subscription.
8. **A terminal is not a pass, and a refusal needs someone to act on it.** A judge-signed
   terminal ships flagged and listed; a fail-closed refusal on a spent ladder deadlocks (ep01
   G-DEAD). -> ep01 retro, failure 8; Process.
9. **An owner ruling retires a sentence.** Grep for it and add it to the retired-phrase list in
   the same commit; on 2026-10-18 five false sentences stood by night. -> ep01 retro (P16).
10. **Green gates prove only the absence of their own faults.** Watch the master, read the
    strips, count what a terminal chose (ep12 shipped over terminals on every taste gate). -> Process.
11. **Where a fault was born decides its cure.** A fault that repeats on a fresh seed is the
    plan's; a panel that carries a fault is the board's; a seed cures a draw. -> Takes; DQ.
12. **H3 obeys direction, not amount**: the cell and the camera verb; point every move at what
    the cell holds. A face under 96 px (3 tokens of 32 px) has no eye the model can draw: plan it
    closer, refine what stays small. -> Takes; The take model follows the planned size; The framing floor.
13. **A place is held by pixels, not words**: one room per setup, a recurring place pinned, a
    state one edit of its master. -> Places; Grids and panels.
14. **The voice is the ruler**: picture cut to the measured voice; every projection reads a
    recorded line's seconds. -> Process; The anime-TV grammar.
15. **Measure where the minutes go before moving them; a free per read is harmful.** -> GPU when
    idle, CPU when rendering.
16. **The owner's eye is the last instrument, never the first.** A run is finished only when the
    cheapest measure of what he will look at passed (face list, QC face strip); a change is safe
    only when replayed on what is delivered. ep01: 6.02 h of machine, then 31 h of wall: two faults
    no gate measured, 11.2 h of agents, 3 launches stopped by unreplayed changes, a 4.68 h silent
    supervisor (silence is a state too). -> The ep01 deadline retro.

## Read by question

- A gate accuses work that looks right: bricks 3 and 7, then the gate's GATES.md row.
- A ladder spends rounds and nothing changes: brick 2; GATES.md "Step caps".
- A picture is wrong in a way no gate saw: bricks 5 and 10; Grids and panels.
- A run stalled, parked or died, or a repair: `OPERATOR.md` (its symptom table); brick 6.
- An owner-reported defect to fix: brick 16; OPERATOR.md "Fixing an owner-reported defect".
- What did the last run teach: the newest dated section of the archive.
